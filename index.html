The Science, Simplified

How it
works

1
Dip the Pen

Dip the tip into your drink for two seconds. Discreet enough that no one around you notices.

2
Micro-Sample Flows In

Three tiny channels draw a micro-sample through the pen. No pumps, just natural capillary action.

3
Color Reaction Happens

If a drug is present, a targeted reaction produces a telltale color, and a tiny optical sensor reads it instantly.

4
Instant Alert

In under five seconds your phone buzzes with the result. If you need them, the contacts you chose are one tap away, quietly and automatically.
